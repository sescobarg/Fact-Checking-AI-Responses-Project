# Sample 014

## sample_id

FC014

## domain

consumer_technology

## user_prompt

Explain what two-factor authentication is and why people use it.

## ai_response_to_evaluate

Two-factor authentication adds a second verification step in addition to a password, such as a code, security key, or app prompt. It can reduce the risk of account takeover when a password is stolen. SMS codes are always the strongest form of two-factor authentication because phone numbers cannot be transferred or intercepted. Hardware security keys are often used for stronger protection against phishing.

## extracted_claims

- `FC014-C01`: Two-factor authentication adds a second verification step in addition to a password.
- `FC014-C02`: Two-factor authentication can reduce the risk of account takeover when a password is stolen.
- `FC014-C03`: SMS codes are always the strongest form of two-factor authentication because phone numbers cannot be transferred or intercepted.

## notes_for_future_verification

- Use cybersecurity guidance from reputable technical or government sources.
- Verify absolute security claims carefully, especially "always" and "cannot."
- Keep future corrections general and avoid account-specific security advice.
