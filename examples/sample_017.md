## user_prompt

Explain what the unemployment rate measures.

## ai_response_to_evaluate

The unemployment rate measures the share of people in the labor force who are unemployed and actively looking for work. People who are retired, full-time students, or not seeking work are generally not counted as unemployed. A low unemployment rate always means wages are rising quickly for all workers.
