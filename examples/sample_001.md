Explain why the first iPhone was important in the history of smartphones.

## ai_response_to_evaluate

Apple released the first iPhone in 2007 after Steve Jobs introduced it at Macworld. It was important because it combined a phone, an iPod, and an internet browser in one touchscreen device. The original iPhone supported 3G networks and launched with the App Store, which immediately allowed third-party developers to sell mobile apps. Its design helped make physical keyboards less common on consumer smartphones.
