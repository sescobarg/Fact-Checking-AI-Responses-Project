## ai_response_to_evaluate

Bus rapid transit, often called BRT, is a public transportation system that uses buses with features intended to make service faster and more reliable. BRT systems may use dedicated lanes, platform-level boarding, and off-board fare collection. The first BRT system in the world opened in New York City in 1995.
