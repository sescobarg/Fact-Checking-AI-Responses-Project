## user_prompt

Explain the difference between weather and climate.

## ai_response_to_evaluate

Weather describes short-term atmospheric conditions such as temperature, rain, wind, and storms. Climate describes average weather patterns over longer periods, commonly measured over 30 years. A cold week in one city can disprove global warming if temperatures are below normal. Human activities such as burning fossil fuels have increased greenhouse gas concentrations in the atmosphere.
