## user_prompt

Explain what makes the James Webb Space Telescope different from Hubble.

## ai_response_to_evaluate

The James Webb Space Telescope observes mainly in infrared light, while the Hubble Space Telescope observes mostly visible and ultraviolet light with some infrared capability. Webb was launched in 2021 and operates near the second Lagrange point, far beyond low Earth orbit. Webb is larger than Hubble and was designed to be serviced regularly by astronauts, just like Hubble.
