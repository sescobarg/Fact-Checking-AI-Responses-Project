## user_prompt

Explain what a data analyst does and what skills are useful for the role.

## ai_response_to_evaluate

A data analyst collects, cleans, and interprets data to help organizations make decisions. Common tools for the role include spreadsheets, SQL, and data visualization software. Most data analyst jobs legally require a master's degree in computer science. Communication skills are important because analysts often explain findings to non-technical stakeholders.
