Travelers from many European countries can enter Iceland through the Schengen Area because Iceland participates in Schengen. Iceland is also a member of the European Union and uses the euro as its official currency. The Blue Lagoon is a natural hot spring located in central Reykjavik, making it easy to visit on foot from most downtown hotels.
