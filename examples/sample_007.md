## user_prompt

Explain the difference between a stock and a bond in simple terms.

## ai_response_to_evaluate

A stock represents partial ownership in a company, while a bond is generally a loan made to a company or government. Stockholders may benefit if the company grows, but the value of a stock can rise or fall. Bonds always pay a fixed interest rate and are risk-free as long as the investor holds them until maturity. In a company bankruptcy, common stockholders are usually paid before bondholders because they own part of the company.
