## user_prompt

Give a general explanation of what copyright protects.

## ai_response_to_evaluate

Copyright protects original creative works such as books, music, films, photographs, and software code. In the United States, copyright generally begins automatically when an eligible work is fixed in a tangible form. Copyright protects ideas themselves, so two people cannot legally write about the same concept. Fair use can allow limited use of copyrighted material, but it depends on context and is not guaranteed just because the use is educational.
