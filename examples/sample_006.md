Give a general explanation of why vaccines are used.

## ai_response_to_evaluate

Vaccines are used to train the immune system to recognize specific diseases before a person is exposed to them. Some vaccines contain weakened or inactive parts of a virus or bacterium, while mRNA vaccines use instructions that help cells produce a harmless protein fragment. Childhood vaccination programs have helped reduce diseases such as measles and polio in many countries. Because vaccines prevent every infection completely, vaccinated people do not need other public health measures during outbreaks.
