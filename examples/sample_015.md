## user_prompt

Explain what dietary fiber is in general terms.

## ai_response_to_evaluate

Dietary fiber is a type of carbohydrate found in plant foods that the body does not fully digest. Foods such as beans, fruits, vegetables, and whole grains can contain fiber. Fiber has no effect on digestion or blood sugar, so nutrition labels do not need to list it. General nutrition guidance often distinguishes between soluble and insoluble fiber.
